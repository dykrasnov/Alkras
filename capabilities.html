Capabilities

Three areas of focus. One standard of precision.

Alkras engineers high-power molecular laser systems for industrial manufacturers, defense programs, and technology partners who require performance that conventional laser platforms cannot deliver. Every system is built around the patented ZORKA platform, developed and refined over 40 years of continuous R&D.

01

Industrial Laser Systems

The ZORKA 2000 delivers continuous output power of up to 2,000W in "A" mode, with a cutting range covering mild steel up to 12mm, stainless steel up to 6mm, and aluminum up to 4mm at excellent edge quality. The platform also cuts chipboard, plywood, plastics, ceramics, composites, glass, and marble — materials where fiber lasers either underperform or require secondary finishing.

Compared directly to CO₂ and fiber laser alternatives, ZORKA systems cut carbon and stainless steels 10 to 20% faster at thicknesses above 1mm, produce mirror-smooth stainless steel edge surfaces, consume half the nitrogen assist gas for stainless cutting, and maintain full optical quality without power degradation over years of operation.

02

Anti-Drone Defense Systems

The ZORKA 1000M is a compact, high-power molecular laser system designed for the neutralization of quadcopters and small to medium unmanned aerial vehicles. It integrates the ZORKA laser generator with an adaptive focus collimator for precision beam delivery at extended ranges.

At 10.6µm wavelength, the ZORKA 1000M operates in a spectral range that UAV materials absorb at 3 to 5 times the rate of 1µm fiber laser systems. This directly reduces the energy required for neutralization at distance. The longer wavelength also experiences less scattering from atmospheric aerosols and dust. The system is eye-safe by spectrum and requires no protective enclosure around the operating area.

Precision Optics & R&D

The ZORKA platform operates across three output modes: TEM00, TEM01*, and the proprietary "A" mode — a precisely tuned combination of TEM00 and TEM01* at optimized proportions that achieves laser efficiency up to 20%. Optical quality is maintained at M² < 1.4 across all three modes regardless of output power level, a characteristic that sets the ZORKA platform apart from comparable industrial systems.

Alkras conducts applied laser R&D in beam shaping, high-power gas laser design, and optical system integration. The team has background in gas-dynamic military laser development, fiber laser systems at the 30–60 kW scale, and quantum computing laser applications. Technology partnership, licensing, and co-development engagements are available for organizations building advanced laser-integrated systems.
