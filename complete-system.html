Complete System

ZORKA 1000M + Visual & Motion Tracking — fully integrated.

The complete Alkras anti-drone platform combines the ZORKA 1000M laser with an integrated electro-optical targeting and motion tracking system — delivering autonomous or operator-assisted target acquisition, tracking, and engagement without requiring a separate sensor suite.

What's included

  • 01 ZORKA 1000M Laser Module — 10.6µm molecular laser with adaptive focus collimator. Physical neutralization to 1,500m, functional effect to 2,000m.
  • 02 Electro-Optical Targeting System — HD and thermal camera suite for target detection and classification at operational range.
  • 03 Motion Tracking & Gimbal — Precision servo-driven platform that acquires and tracks UAV targets in real time, keeping the beam on target throughout the engagement.
  • 04 Command & Control Interface — Operator console with manual override, engagement authorization, and threat logging. Integrates with existing C2 infrastructure.
  • 05 Ground Deployment Package — Mobile platform with power conditioning, weatherproof enclosure, and rapid setup capability for fixed or mobile deployment.
