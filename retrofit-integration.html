Retrofit

Upgrading your existing system

If you have an operating cutting system with an aging laser source, Alkras offers direct retrofit. We replace the laser generator with a ZORKA-series laser and make any necessary modifications — we have the components and the experience to adapt the gantry and motion control system to the new laser source.

The result is a system that cuts faster, cleaner, and more reliably than before, without the cost of replacing the entire machine. Integration is handled by our engineering team and includes commissioning, calibration, and operator handover.

  • → Compatible with most standard CNC gantry platforms
  • → Gantry and drives adapted to the new laser source as needed
  • → Full commissioning and performance validation on-site
  • → Immediate improvement in cut speed, quality, and material range
