ZORKA 2000

A new generation of molecular CO₂ laser — engineered for industrial precision.

The ZORKA 2000 is the flagship industrial laser system from Alkras, delivering up to 2,000W of continuous output power in proprietary "A" mode. It is built on a patented integral optical resonator with combination-plasma excitation, a fundamental design difference from conventional CO₂ and fiber lasers that produces measurably superior cutting results across every material class.

The ZORKA platform operates at 10.6µm wavelength — the optimal range for materials processing. At this wavelength, the laser achieves up to 18% efficiency, maintains beam quality at M² < 1.4 across all output modes, and does not degrade in power or optical performance over years of continuous operation.

Beam Mode Analysis

Three operating modes — optimized for every task.

The ZORKA 2000 produces three distinct beam modes selectable by configuration. Each mode delivers a different intensity profile and beam diameter, allowing the system to be tuned for precision cutting, high-power throughput, or maximum efficiency depending on the material and application.

TEM₀₀ Mode — 1,200 W

Fundamental Gaussian Mode

The highest beam quality mode. A single, tightly focused Gaussian spot with M² < 1.2 and 15mm output diameter. Optimal for precision cutting, thin materials, and applications where minimum kerf width and edge quality are the priority.

TEM₀₁* Mode — 1,500 W

Ring-Mode Profile

A donut-shaped intensity ring with M² < 1.8 and 18mm output diameter. Higher power than TEM₀₀ with distributed energy across the beam perimeter — preferred for thicker metals and applications where heat distribution across the cut zone improves edge quality.

"A" Mode — 2,000 W

Maximum Power Mode

Full 2,000W output with M² < 2.5 and 20mm beam diameter — proprietary to the ZORKA platform. Used for maximum-throughput cutting of thick steel and aluminum, and for applications where raw power density is the determining factor in cut quality and speed.
