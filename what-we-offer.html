Industrial

Laser systems for cutting and processing.

Laser Source

ZORKA 2000

Standalone molecular CO₂ laser generator, up to 2,000W continuous output. Zero power degradation over its service life. Cuts steel, stainless, aluminum, glass, ceramics, wood, and more. Available in TEM00 (1,200W), TEM01* (1,500W), and "A" mode (2,000W).

Laser System

MALIBU 1212

Vertical die board laser cutting system by ANSEAT Lasertechnik (Germany). 300W CO₂ laser, 25–30 m/hr cutting speed, ±0.05mm slot depth accuracy, ±0.01mm positioning precision. Autonomous sheet loading and real-time slot width auto-correction — a capability no competing machine offers.

Retrofit

Laser Retrofit

Replacement of an aging laser source on an existing CNC cutting table with an Alkras AL-series unit. Keep your gantry, table, chiller, and motion control — replace only the laser. Engineered and commissioned by the Alkras team.

Laser Module

ZORKA 1000M

Compact, field-deployable molecular laser system for UAV neutralization. Physical neutralization at 1,500m, functional effect to 2,000m, 3–5 second engagement time. Available as a standalone module for integration into existing defense infrastructure, C2 systems, or custom mounting platforms.
