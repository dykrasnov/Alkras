Defense

Anti-drone laser systems

Laser & Targeting System

ZORKA-M

ZORKA-M is the defense variant of the ZORKA series, purpose-built to counter UAVs under real operational conditions. The laser is paired with a dedicated targeting and beam-focusing system. Physical neutralization at 1,500m, functional effect to 2,000m, engagement time of a few seconds. The most efficient and compact system on the market — at a fraction of the cost of comparable alternatives.

Anti-Drone System
