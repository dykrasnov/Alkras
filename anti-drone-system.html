Why Molecular Lasers Beat Fiber for Anti-Drone

The physics of 10.6µm versus 1µm, and why it matters in the field.

  • 01 3 to 5× higher absorption by UAV materials. Drone plastic, foam, and composite materials absorb 10.6µm laser energy at 3 to 5 times the rate of 1µm fiber laser systems, meaning less power is needed to achieve the same destructive effect at distance.
  • 02 Minimal atmospheric turbulence impact. The 10.6µm beam is far less affected by atmospheric distortion than shorter wavelengths, allowing stable, accurate delivery at extended range without adaptive optics systems (COAT).
  • 03 Less energy loss to aerosol scattering. The longer wavelength experiences significantly less scattering from dust, smoke, and atmospheric aerosols than 1µm fiber systems, a critical advantage in real operational environments.
  • 04 Eye-safe by spectrum. The 10.6µm emission is naturally eye-safe. No protective enclosure or restricted zone is required around the operating position, simplifying deployment in field and urban settings.
  • 05 Compact and field-deployable. At 1.8 × 1.0 × 1.3 m and 230 kg, the ZORKA 1000M is designed for ground deployment. No specialized vehicle or fixed infrastructure is required for operation.
Option 1: Standalone Laser

ZORKA 1000M: the standalone laser weapon module.

The ZORKA 1000M is the deployable laser generator at the core of the Alkras anti-drone platform. It is available as a standalone unit for integration into existing defense infrastructure, C2 systems, or custom mounting solutions. Operates at 10.6µm (eye-safe spectrum) with a continuous output beam and adaptive focus collimator spanning 50 to 5,000 meters.

Designed for defense contractors, system integrators, and military procurement programs that require a proven high-energy laser source without the full tracking package. The ZORKA 1000M ships as a field-ready module ready for third-party platform integration.
