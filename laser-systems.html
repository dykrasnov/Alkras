Vertical System

A ready solution today: vertical die board cutting

In partnership with a European manufacturer, we offer a ready-made vertical system, integrated with our laser sources, for cutting die board and sheet metal. The vertical layout occupies significantly less floor space than horizontal cutting tables with the same working area, and provides fully autonomous sheet loading and unloading for unattended production.

The system's standout feature is real-time slot-width auto-correction: it continuously monitors and adjusts kerf geometry during cutting, delivering consistently high edge quality across the entire production run — a capability no other machine on the market offers.
