MALIBU 1212

The vertical die board laser. Built by ANSEAT Lasertechnik, Germany.

The MALIBU 1212 is a vertical die board laser cutting system produced in partnership with ANSEAT Lasertechnik (Germany) — a specialist manufacturer with decades of precision machine engineering behind it. It handles all standard plywood sheet sizes in a compact floor footprint, with fully autonomous sheet loading and unloading for unattended batch production.

What sets it apart from every competing die board machine is real-time slot width auto-correction during the cut. The system continuously monitors and adjusts kerf geometry as the laser runs — a capability no other machine on the market offers — ensuring consistent, press-ready board quality across the entire sheet and the full production run.
