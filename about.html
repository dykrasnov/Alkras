About Alkras

Four decades of laser physics. Seven patents. Proven performance in industrial and defense applications.

Alkras was built around a single engineering conviction: that molecular laser technology could outperform what the industry had settled for. Founded by Alexander Krasnov — a physicist who has worked in laser development since 1982 — the company has spent decades turning that conviction into patented systems with documented, measurable results across industrial cutting and directed-energy defense.

Industry Recognition

The ZORKA platform in the trade press.

Technology Spotlight

"Light, compact, multikilobawatt CO₂ laser fits on a gantry"

Published in fabrication industry trade media

Greiner Industries — a fabricator known for the world's largest steel rolling capacity — selected the Malibu CO₂ laser system for their cutting operation. The feature documents how the ZORKA-derived molecular CO₂ laser architecture achieves superior cut quality in a compact, gantry-mounted format previously unattainable with conventional CO₂ technology.

The article highlights the laser's performance mechanism: high energy density at the beam surface drives fast material penetration, while the energy distribution near the beam edge produces clean, consistent cut quality across material thicknesses — a combination that competing fiber and conventional CO₂ platforms struggle to replicate.

"High energy at the surface helps penetrate the material quickly while the high energy near the edge of the laser diameter helps produce a clean cut."

Why Alkras

What sets the ZORKA platform apart from every alternative on the market.

01

Superior Cutting Speed

ZORKA molecular lasers cut carbon steel and stainless steel 10 to 20% faster than fiber lasers at thicknesses above 1mm. Speed advantage compounds at greater material thicknesses.

02

Mirror-Quality Edge Finish

Stainless steel cuts produced by the ZORKA platform achieve a mirror-smooth surface finish. Fiber lasers at equivalent power cannot replicate this on stainless — secondary finishing is typically required.

03

Universal Material Cutting

Unlike fiber lasers which struggle with non-metals, the ZORKA platform cuts steel, aluminum, glass, ceramics, composites, plastics, plywood, marble, and more — all at high quality.

04

No Power Degradation

Fiber laser output power degrades over years of operation. ZORKA molecular lasers maintain full optical quality and power output across the entire operational lifespan of the system.

05

Half the Assist Gas Consumption

ZORKA systems consume half the nitrogen assist gas compared to fiber lasers when cutting stainless steel — a direct, measurable reduction in operating costs at industrial scale.

06

Eye-Safe Spectrum

The 10.6µm emission wavelength is eye-safe by spectrum. No protective sarcophagus or enclosure is required around the operating area, simplifying installation and facility integration.

Head Team

Three engineers. One standard for precision work.

AK

Alexander Krasnov

Inventor & Founder

Alexander has worked in laser development since 1982. Educated at Moscow State University, he holds 45 years of continuous professional experience across technical implementation, product development, and R&D. His scientific depth spans aerodynamics, wave and nonlinear optics, and high-power gas laser engineering. He is the inventor of the compact ultra-high-power molecular laser at the core of the ZORKA platform, developed for both military air-defense and civilian industrial applications.

YK

Yuri Krasnov

Co-Founder & Commercial Director

Yuri brings 30 years of international business, sales, and technical marketing experience across CO₂, solid-state, fiber, picosecond, and direct diode-pumped laser systems. He worked at IPG Photonics developing 30–60 kW laser systems and at TeraDiode/Panasonic on direct diode-pumped platforms. He and Alexander collaborated on the R&D and design of gas-dynamic military lasers early in their careers, and Yuri continues to lead Alkras's commercial development and international partnerships.

What we're building toward

Alkras is building toward a future where precision molecular laser technology is the standard, not the exception, for industrial processing and specialized defense applications.

Every system Alkras engineers is held to the same standard: beam quality that does not drift, power output that does not degrade over years of operation, and cutting performance that outpaces fiber lasers on the materials that matter. The ZORKA platform achieves efficiencies up to 18% and maintains optical quality across TEM00, TEM01*, and "A" mode output regardless of power level.

The long-term goal is to establish ZORKA-series molecular lasers as a recognized platform for high-performance industrial cutting and anti-drone defense systems on an international scale. Alkras is actively seeking technology partners, contract manufacturers, and strategic investors to bring that work to more markets.
