About Alkras

Engineering built on precision, run like a family business — because it is one.

Alkras was built around a single engineering conviction: that molecular laser technology could outperform what the industry had settled for. Founded by Alexander Krasnov — a physicist who has worked in laser development since 1982 — the company has spent decades turning that conviction into patented systems with measurable results.

Our Story

Four decades of laser physics, built into a family-run engineering firm.

Alkras traces its roots to Alexander Krasnov's work in high-power gas laser development, beginning in the early 1980s at Moscow State University. What started as scientific research evolved into a focused engineering practice: designing compact, high-efficiency molecular laser systems that challenge the assumptions built into conventional CO₂ and fiber laser technology. The result is the ZORKA series — a proprietary laser platform developed and patented by the Krasnov family.

The team that built Alkras is the same team that runs it. Yuri Krasnov brings 30 years of international business and laser commercialization experience, including applied work at IPG Photonics on 30–60 kW systems and at TeraDiode/Panasonic on direct diode-pumped lasers. Daniel Krasnov contributes expertise in object recognition and mechanical engineering, skill sets that directly support the precision targeting demands of Alkras's specialized programs. This is not a company built around a pitch deck. It is built around the work.

What we're building toward

Alkras is building toward a future where precision molecular laser technology is the standard — not the exception — for industrial processing and specialized defense applications.

Every system Alkras engineers is held to the same standard: beam quality that does not drift, power output that does not degrade over years of operation, and cutting performance that outpaces fiber lasers on the materials that matter. The ZORKA platform achieves efficiencies up to 18% and maintains optical quality across TEM00, TEM01*, and "A" mode output regardless of power level. That is not a specification written for a datasheet — it is the threshold the team works to.

The long-term goal is to establish ZORKA-series molecular lasers as a recognized platform for high-performance industrial cutting and anti-drone defense systems on an international scale. Alkras is actively seeking technology partners, contract manufacturers, and strategic investors to bring that work to more markets. The technology is proven, the IP is protected, and the team has the track record to back it.

The team

Three engineers. One standard for precision work.

AK

Alexander Krasnov

Inventor & Founder

Alexander has worked in laser development since 1982. Educated at Moscow State University, he holds 45 years of continuous professional experience across technical implementation, product development, and R&D. His scientific depth spans aerodynamics, wave and nonlinear optics, and high-power gas laser engineering. He is the inventor of the compact ultra-high-power molecular laser at the core of the ZORKA platform, developed for both military air-defense and civilian industrial applications.

YK

Yuri Krasnov

Co-Founder & Commercial Director

Yuri brings 30 years of international business, sales, and technical marketing experience across CO₂, solid-state, fiber, picosecond, and direct diode-pumped laser systems. He worked at IPG Photonics developing 30–60 kW laser systems and at TeraDiode/Panasonic on direct diode-pumped platforms. He and Alexander collaborated on the R&D and design of gas-dynamic military lasers early in their careers, and Yuri continues to lead Alkras's commercial development and international partnerships.

DK

Daniel Krasnov

Engineer — Object Recognition & Mechanical Systems

Daniel is the youngest member of the Alkras team, and the years he hasn't spent in the field he has more than compensated for in capability. He specializes in object recognition engineering and mechanical systems design — disciplines that converge directly on the targeting and tracking demands of Alkras's anti-drone programs. His work on automated detection and precision mechanical integration is a meaningful technical contribution to the next generation of the ZORKA platform.
