Why Alkras

The superior laser platform — on performance, cost, and longevity.

The simplicity of Alkras technology allows manufacturers to substantially cut the cost of production and increase profit margins. The ZORKA molecular laser platform is extremely low in cost to maintain — no fiber source degradation, no expensive replacement cycles, and a design architecture that holds full output power indefinitely.

The ZORKA platform cuts 10–20% faster on steel than fiber lasers at equal power, produces a mirror-smooth stainless finish at half the nitrogen consumption, and handles materials fiber systems simply cannot — glass, ceramics, composites, wood, and plastics. It operates on an eye-safe 10.6µm spectrum requiring no protective enclosures, and delivers the lowest 5-year total cost of ownership across system cost, maintenance, and operations combined. One platform. Every material. Full performance for the life of the machine.
