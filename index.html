Who We Are

A family-run engineering firm with an exacting standard for work.

Alkras was founded on a straightforward premise: laser systems only matter if they perform exactly as specified, every time. Built around the patented ZORKA series, our molecular laser platform was developed over 40 years of continuous R&D by founder Alexander Krasnov.

We engineer for industrial manufacturers who demand cutting performance fiber lasers cannot match, and for defense programs that require precision energy delivery at distance.
