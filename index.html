About the Company

Homegrown engineering. Patented technology, world-class standard.

For more than 35 years, Alexander Krasnov has repeatedly driven new waves of advancement in laser technology. His work — from the laser sources themselves to concentrating the beam over long distances under real atmospheric conditions — has proven its superiority over competitors again and again, a result no one else has yet managed to replicate.

His latest development is the ZORKA series of lasers, integrated into both industrial and defense systems. Today Alkras offers laser sources, laser cutting systems, retrofit of existing installations, and anti-drone defense systems — and continues to develop new technologies, pushing the boundaries of what's possible in laser engineering.
