Precision engineering, built for what comes next.

Alkras designs, builds, and refines high-power molecular laser systems for industrial cutting and specialized defense applications. Engineered by a family of scientists who treat precision as the baseline, not the pitch.

Sec. 01 — Who We Are

A family-run engineering firm with an exacting standard for work.

Alkras was founded on a straightforward premise: laser systems only matter if they perform exactly as specified, every time. That standard shapes every project, from early design through final testing and delivery.

The company is built around the ZORKA series, a proprietary line of high-power molecular lasers developed and patented by founder Alexander Krasnov over 40 years of continuous laser R&D. The systems are engineered for industrial materials processing and specialized anti-drone defense applications.

Engineering capabilities built around precision.

Three areas of focus, each held to the same exacting tolerance.

01

Industrial Laser Systems

ZORKA-series CO₂ molecular lasers from 1,000 to 5,000W for precision cutting of metals, composites, glass, ceramics, and wood. Up to 12mm mild steel, 6mm stainless, 4mm aluminum at mirror-smooth edge quality.
02

Anti-Drone Defense Systems

The ZORKA 1000M laser system neutralizes quadcopters and small to mid-size drones at distances up to 1,500m physically and 2,000m functionally. Typical engagement time: 3 to 5 seconds.
03

Precision Optics & R&D

Beam engineering across TEM00, TEM01*, and proprietary "A" mode output. Optical system design and applied laser R&D for custom programs and technology partners.
