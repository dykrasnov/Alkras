Partnerships

Building the next generation of laser technology together.

We invite technology companies, industrial manufacturers, research organizations, investors, and strategic partners to discuss collaboration. Alkras is actively seeking partners to develop and commercialize advanced laser systems for industry and defense at scale.

What We're Building Toward

Scalable, commercially viable laser platforms for industry and defense.

We are seeking partners in technology, manufacturing, and strategic development for the production and commercialization of advanced ZORKA-series laser systems for industrial applications and anti-drone defense systems.

Our goal is to build reliable, scalable, and commercially effective laser platforms combining high performance, modular architecture, operational reliability, and cost-competitive manufacturing technology. The IP is protected, the technology is proven, and the team has the engineering depth to execute.

Слева направо: Валентин Гапонцев, генеральный директор IPG Photonics; Александр Краснов, изобретатель молекулярного лазера; Вильям Шайнер, коммерческий директор IPG Photonics.

Partnership Models

Six ways to work with Alkras.

Depending on your capabilities and strategic goals, collaboration can take any of the following forms.

01

Technology Partnership

Joint development, licensing, technology transfer, or integration of proprietary ZORKA laser components into your systems. For companies building laser-integrated products who want access to patented molecular laser technology.

02

Contract Manufacturing

Production of ZORKA laser sources, subsystems, or complete systems to agreed technical specifications. For manufacturers with the capacity and precision to produce high-power laser equipment at scale.

03

Joint Development Program

Collaborative design, prototyping, testing, and commercialization of new laser platforms. For organizations that want to co-invest in the development of next-generation ZORKA systems for specific markets.

04

Strategic Joint Venture

Formation of a dedicated entity for production and product development targeting defined markets. For partners seeking a long-term, structured commercial relationship with shared ownership and revenue.

05

System Integration Partnership

Integration of ZORKA laser sources with optical, mechanical, and electronic systems, automation, and software. For system integrators building complete laser workstations, cutting machines, or defense platforms.

06

Commercial Distribution

Joint market entry, sales, service, technical support, and regional representation. For established distributors or sales organizations in laser equipment, industrial machinery, or defense procurement.

What you get access to.

  • IP 7 issued patents covering molecular laser architecture, beam engineering, and system design. Protected and available for licensing or joint development.
  • R&D 40+ years of continuous laser R&D by a team that has worked on gas-dynamic military lasers, 30–60 kW fiber systems at IPG Photonics, and direct diode-pumped platforms at TeraDiode/Panasonic.
  • Tech A proven, field-demonstrated laser platform with documented performance advantages over CO₂, fiber, and disk laser alternatives, in both industrial cutting and anti-drone applications.
